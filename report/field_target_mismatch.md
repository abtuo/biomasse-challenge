## Interpretation

The median nearest-shot distance is 171.3 m. For the 66 plots within 100 m, median field minus nearest GEDI AGBD is 5.10 Mg/ha and Spearman association is 0.410.
Nearby does not mean support-matched: plot area is absent, GEDI footprints and plot geometry differ, acquisition dates differ, and GEDI AGBD is itself model-derived. These comparisons separate possible target/support mismatch from map error descriptively; they do not validate one target as truth at the other's support.
